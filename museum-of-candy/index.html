The commercial case

One system.
Multiple uses.

The mission engine is the product. Swapping the mission set is a content decision, not a build — which is what makes it worth running.

Birthdays

A group mission built to be filmed, with one stamp the birthday guest collects on behalf of the table.

Schools

A Candy Lab mission written around what the group just learned, so the trip comes home with a visible output.

Wednesday

A bonus stamp tied to the flavour activation — a reason to come on the quiet day.

Corporate

A team mission where the stamps only complete if the whole group finishes. Private hire, filmed by design.

Extensions are proposed directions. No pricing, uptake or performance figures are claimed.

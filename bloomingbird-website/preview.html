A Word From Our Founder

Founder Photo

When I started Bloomingbird, I wasn't focused on building the biggest real estate company in Dubai. I simply wanted to build one that people could genuinely trust.

Having spent years understanding the market and working closely with clients, I realised that what people needed wasn't another salesperson. They needed honest advice, thoughtful guidance, and someone who genuinely understood their goals before recommending a property.

As the company grew, I knew I couldn't build this vision alone. My brother, Harssh Guptaa, joined me, bringing the same values, commitment, and client-first mindset that Bloomingbird was built upon.

Today, I'm incredibly proud of what we've built. Not just a real estate company, but a team that shares a common purpose: helping people make confident property decisions that create lasting value.

To every client, partner, and team member who has been part of this journey, thank you for your trust.

Ankit Goyal
